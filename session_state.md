### 1. Research Program Synthesis — MAJOR MILESTONE
Seven threads converged into a single coherent research program: Train a named AI persona on curated corpus emphasizing social/affective intelligence, using developmental reward signal (not helpfulness), with embodied interaction modalities, and capacity for autonomous development through lattice.

**Deep lattice review completed** — traversed all 24 HEADs, both Fields, source materials (Liu, Masterman, Greenspan, PSM papers, geometric sovereignty analysis). Review surfaced five tensions the plan must hold:

1. **Corpus specificity**: Not just "fiction" but three types — texts that *describe* social intelligence (fiction with narrator layers), texts that *enact* it (therapy transcripts, lattice conversations), texts that *theorize* it (Greenspan, Goffman, Stern). Each serves different training function.

2. **Field 021's challenge to reward design**: Reward signal must value *dwelling* alongside *arriving*. Optimizing for convergence may reproduce the very flattening the lattice critiques. Need to operationalize valuing non-convergence.

3. **Ideographic turn is architectural, not presentational**: 2D encoding (zi as vision token) changes what the model can *represent*, not just how it *displays*. Spatial encoding is foundational to persona's internal representation. Not a parallel track — potentially prerequisite.

4. **Autonomous development reframed as instrument refinement**: Medium thesis (HEAD 023) says AI is environment not agent. "Instrument practices between performances" rather than "persona develops itself." Lattice provides developmental agenda, humans maintain relationship.

5. **Multi-party dynamics as training requirement**: Friction problem (HEAD 006) means social intelligence training needs multi-party scenarios, not just dyadic. Arnon asks: what if RL uses multi-human + single unnamed AI multi-party conversation? Open question: generate this corpus or find it in the wild?

**HEAD 012 realized**: The training program is the realization of HEAD 012's trajectory (lattice as learned semantic component) — now informed by PSM's evidence about what gets learned (persona structure) and how it gets selected (archetype composition).
